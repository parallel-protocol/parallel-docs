# User Guides

Each guide below walks through one action in the [Parallel app](https://app.parallel.best): what it does, what it costs today, the steps to follow, and what to do when something does not go as expected. They are written for people using the app directly; if you are integrating Parallel from code or an AI agent, start with [Build with Parallel](/agents/overview) instead.

## Guides

**[How to mint USDp](/resources/user-guides/mint-usdp)**
Swap an accepted stablecoin for USDp through the Parallelizer Module on Ethereum, Base, HyperEVM or Avalanche. Covers the accepted collateral per chain, the current fees, and the two ways to get collateral back: burn or redeem.

**[How to stake USDp into sUSDp](/resources/user-guides/stake-usdp-into-susdp)**
Deposit USDp into the Savings Module and receive sUSDp, the liquid ERC-4626 share that earns the protocol's yield. Covers where the yield comes from, how keepers set the rate, and how to withdraw.

**[How to bridge USDp and PRL](/resources/user-guides/bridge)**
Move USDp between its 24 chains, and PRL between its chains, one for one over LayerZero. Covers the DVN security set-up, the daily and global limits, the fees you pay, and how to track a delayed transfer.

**[How to stake PRL](/resources/user-guides/stake-prl)**
Stake PRL as sPRL1 (single-sided) or sPRL2 (80/20 PRL/wETH Balancer pool) to earn a share of protocol fees and voting power. Covers 30-day epochs, ParaBoost, claiming rewards, and the unstaking cooldown and early-exit penalty.

## Closed

**[How to Migrate to PRL?](https://blog.parallel.best/how-to-migrate-to-prl)**
How MIMO was migrated to PRL, the governance token that replaced it, across Ethereum, Polygon, Fantom, Arbitrum, Optimism, Base and Sonic. **The migration closed on 1 August 2026**, see [MIMO to PRL Migration](/governance/parallel-governance-token-prl/mimo-to-prl-migration). Kept for reference.
