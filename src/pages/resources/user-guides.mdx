# User Guides

### Where to start using Parallel  ?

Everything you need to know to use the protocol can be found below.

**[How to Mint USDp?](https://blog.parallel.best/how-to-mint-usdp)**
Minting and burning USDp through the Parallelizer Module: which stablecoins are accepted as collateral, the fees and exposure limits the DAO sets for each, and the steps to swap collateral for USDp — or USDp back — in the app. Minting pauses for a collateral that has reached its maximum exposure.

**[How to Stake USDp into sUSDp?](https://blog.parallel.best/how-to-stake-usdp-into-susdp)**
Staking USDp into sUSDp, the savings vault whose yield comes from the revenue the protocol earns on the assets backing USDp. Covers depositing and withdrawing from the Earn section, and how the rate is updated by keepers. There is no staking fee and sUSDp stays liquid.

**[How to Bridge Parallel Tokens?](https://blog.parallel.best/how-to-bridge-parallel-tokens)**
Moving PRL, PAR and paUSD between supported chains with Parallel's LayerZero-based bridge: choosing the origin and destination chains, sending 1:1 with no protocol-level fee, and setting a recipient address other than your own.

**[How to Stake PRL?](https://blog.parallel.best/how-to-stake-prl)**
The two ways to stake PRL — sPRL1 single-sided, or sPRL2 in the 80/20 PRL/wETH Balancer pool — locked into 30-day epochs. Explains the ParaBoost score, and how governance weight and the share of protocol fees differ between the two.

**[How to Migrate to PRL?](https://blog.parallel.best/how-to-migrate-to-prl)**
Migrating MIMO to PRL, the governance token that replaced it, across Ethereum, Polygon, Fantom, Arbitrum, Optimism, Base and Sonic, including how vMIMO is withdrawn first. The guide states the migration was due to close on 1 August 2026 and is irreversible.
