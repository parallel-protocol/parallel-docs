# Epoch Concept

The epoch is the central unit of time in the tokenomics. An epoch is one calendar month, so it lasts 28, 29, 30 or 31 days depending on the month. It is used to determine the [ParaBoost](/governance/parallel-governance-token-prl/tokenomics/paraboost) calculation period and to manage the [fee sharing](/governance/parallel-governance-token-prl/tokenomics/fee-distribution) distribution to sPRL1 and sPRL2 holders.

On-chain, the `RewardMerkleDistributor` enforces an `EPOCH_LENGTH` of 28 days as the minimum window between two epochs, which every calendar month satisfies.

:::info
The cooldown applied when unstaking sPRL1 and sPRL2 is **not** one epoch. It is a separate, fixed period of 28 days, held per staking contract as `timeLockDuration` and changeable by governance within a 1 to 365 day range. See [Staking Mechanisms](/governance/parallel-governance-token-prl/tokenomics/staking-mechanisms).
:::
