## How AI agents pay for APIs

AI agents can't use credit cards. They can't fill out checkout forms. They need a protocol-level way to pay APIs autonomously — one request, one payment, no human in the loop.

[x402](/agents/x402) is that protocol. An HTTP-native `402 Payment Required` flow: the API server requires payment, the agent signs a stablecoin transfer, the facilitator verifies and settles it, and the agent gets the response. Settles on-chain in seconds, works at sub-cent prices.

Parallel runs an [x402 facilitator](/agents/x402/concepts) that:

- Accepts USDp, USDC, and sUSDp
- Sponsors the gas on Base, HyperEVM, and Avalanche
- Charges no platform fee
- Settles directly to the merchant's wallet — no Parallel account required

[Read the x402 quickstart →](/agents/x402/quickstart)

## Talk to the protocol from your AI

The [Parallel MCP server](/agents/mcp) connects Claude, Cursor or any MCP client to the protocol: live data, quotes, and ready-to-sign transactions — zero config, and it never touches your keys.

## Or from the terminal

The [Parallel CLI](/agents/cli) puts the same surface in your shell and your scripts: live data as JSON, transactions built, signed and broadcast from the command line — with the wallet strictly opt-in.
