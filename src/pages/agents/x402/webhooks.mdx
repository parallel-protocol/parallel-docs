# Webhooks

Webhook delivery for merchants is **on the roadmap**.

Until then, you can confirm payments in three ways:

- **Read the `payment-response` header** of the paid response. The middleware adds it after your handler has answered, so your handler does not see it: read it from the response on its way out. It holds the `txHash`, the `route`, the `payer` and the `amount` charged, and `amountSettled` on metered routes ([schema](/agents/x402/schemas)). If you build on `createPaymentGate`, `settleVerified` returns the same confirmation as an object.
- **Ask the facilitator.** `GET https://agents.parallel.best/x402/verify?txHash=<hash>&chain=<slug>` says in `x402Payment` whether the transaction is a payment the facilitator settled, and in `txMined` whether it was mined and succeeded. Without `chain`, a hash the facilitator has no record of gets a `400` with `MISSING_CHAIN`.
- **Watch your `payTo` wallet on-chain** for incoming transfers.

When webhooks ship, this page will document the event types, payload format, and signature verification.
