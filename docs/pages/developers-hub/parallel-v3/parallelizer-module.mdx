Known Issue:

* parallelizer/facets/Swapper.sol: Incorrect handling of deadline causes reverts and breaks documented swap behavior. The Parallelizer Module is a licensed fork of the Angle Protocol's Transmuter contract, and the comment originates from the original Angle codebase. However, during our audit with Bailsec, a vulnerability related to a 0 deadline was identified, and they recommended blocking it to protect users. The change has been implemented the original comment has not been removed.
:::
